= Exercise 1: Violations and forensic data
:navtitle: Exercise 1: Violations and forensics

Estimated time: 5 minutes.

A violation in RHACS is a record of a specific moment when a policy criterion was met by an object in your cluster — an image, a deployment configuration, or a runtime process.
The Violations page is the event stream for *deploy-time* and *runtime* findings.
Build-time failures surface in your CI pipeline instead — they never land here.

== Task 1.1: Inspect a deploy-time violation

. Open the RHACS Console from the Showroom *Red Hat Advanced Cluster Security* tab.
. From the left menu, navigate to *Violations*.
. Locate a violation for the *Fixable Severity at Least Important* policy.
. Look at the different columns available, then click any violation.
. Review the information inside it (deployment detail, policy definition, and remediation).
. When finished, navigate back to the *Violations* list.

== Task 1.2: Inspect a runtime violation

. In the *Policy* filter bar, type `Ubuntu` and select *Ubuntu Package Manager Execution*.
. Notice that this is a *runtime* violation.
. Open the violation and check the forensic details available:
** Process name
** Arguments passed to it
** UID it ran under
** Container ID
** Exact timestamp
. Open the policy that triggered this violation.
. Note the *Guidance* section — it tells the development team exactly what to remove from the Containerfile to prevent this from happening again.

A package manager has no legitimate purpose in a container that ships with all its dependencies.
Removing it from the image eliminates an entire class of attacker capability in the next build.

== Key takeaways

* Policies span four lifecycle stages — build, deploy, build and deploy, and runtime — but the Violations page stores deploy-time and runtime events.
* Build-time enforcement stops bad images in CI before they are promoted; deploy-time and runtime enforcement catch what reaches the cluster.
* Runtime violations include process-level detail that maps directly to incident response workflows.
* The Guidance section converts a runtime alert into a specific, actionable Containerfile improvement.
