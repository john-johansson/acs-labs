= Exercise 1: Violations and built-in policy inspection
:navtitle: Exercise 1: Violations

Estimated time: 10 minutes.

== Task 1.1: Inspect active violations

. Open the RHACS Console from the Showroom *Red Hat Advanced Cluster Security* tab.
. From the left menu, open *Violations*.
. Filter:
** *Lifecycle Stage:* `Deploy`
** *Severity:* `High`, `Critical`
. Open an active violation (for example *Fixable Severity at least Important* or *Privileged Container*) and review deployment detail, policy definition, and remediation.

== Task 1.2: Explore the policy library

. Navigate to *Platform Configuration → Policy Management*.
. Search for `Fixable`.
. Open *Fixable Severity at least Important* (enabled by default) and optionally *Fixable CVSS >= 7* (disabled by default).
. Note:
** *Rationale:* prevent promotion of workloads with known important/critical fixable vulnerabilities.
** *Response method:* most built-in policies start as *Inform only*. Confirm Deploy enforcement is off before Exercise 2.
