= Exercise 2: Automated vulnerability reports
:navtitle: Exercise 2: Automated vulnerability reports

include::partial$session2-ui.adoc[]

Estimated time: 15 minutes.

RHACS can generate vulnerability reports in PDF and CSV format.
Reports replace the old workflow of manually exporting, zipping, and emailing CSV files to tenant leads.

On this Showroom, use *on-demand download* as the success path.
A scheduled email report needs an email notifier under *Delivery*.
Without SMTP, leave *Delivery* empty (no destination, no schedule) and generate the report manually after you save the configuration.

By the end of this exercise you will:

* Walk through the five-step report wizard (Details, Resources, Filters, Delivery, Review).
* Scope a report to a single namespace using *Custom scope*.
* Generate and download a PDF or CSV report on demand without SMTP.
* Understand when to use on-demand download vs scheduled email delivery.

== Task 2.1: Open Vulnerability Reporting

. In the RHACS Console, expand *Vulnerability Management* and click *Reports*.
. Confirm you land on *Image vulnerability reports* with the *Report configurations* tab selected.
. An empty list is expected on a fresh lab (*No vulnerability reports yet*).
+
image::s2/s2-02-reports-list.png[Empty Image vulnerability reports list with Create report button,width=720]

== Task 2.2: Create a downloadable report configuration

The create flow is a five-step wizard: *Details* → *Resources* → *Filters* → *Delivery* → *Review*.
Work through each step below. Do not look for a single flat form with all fields on one page.

=== Step 1: Details

. Click *Create report*.
. On the *Details* step, fill in:
+
[cols="1,2",options="header"]
|===
|Field |Value

|Name
|`Lab - Payments Vulnerability Report`

|Description
|`Fixable Important and Critical CVEs for payments (on-demand download).`
|===
. Click *Next*.

=== Step 2: Resources

. On *Resources*, open *Image type* and select *Deployed images* (the toggle shows a count badge of `1` when selected).
. Under *Scope method*, select *Custom scope* (preferred over *Collection scope*; collections are being retired).
. In the custom scope filter row:
.. Set the entity dropdown to *Namespace* (it may default to *Cluster*).
.. Leave the attribute as *Name*.
.. In *Filter results by Namespace name*, type `payments`, then select the `payments` suggestion so a chip appears (for example `Namespace name "payments"`).
+
image::s2/s2-02-create-resources.png[Resources step with Deployed images Custom scope and payments namespace chip,width=720]
+
IMPORTANT: You must select at least one *Image type* before *Next* enables the rest of the wizard. If you see *Select at least one image type*, reopen *Image type* and choose *Deployed images*.
. Click *Next*.

=== Step 3: Filters

. On *Filters*, confirm the default chips already match the lab goal:
** *CVEs discovered in image since:* *All time*
** *Vulnerability state:* *Observed*
** *CVE severity:* *Critical* and *Important*
** *CVE status:* *Fixable*
+
image::s2/s2-02-create-filters.png[Filters step with Observed Critical Important Fixable defaults,width=720]
. Leave these defaults. Click *Next*.

=== Step 4: Delivery

. On *Delivery*, leave the step empty.
. Do *not* click *Add destination* (that path needs an email notifier / SMTP).
+
IMPORTANT: ACS rejects a report that has a schedule or email destination without a working notifier. Weekly email digests are the production pattern after SMTP is configured. This lab proves report content with on-demand download instead.
. Click *Next*.

=== Step 5: Review and save

. On *Review*, confirm the summary looks like this:
** *Name:* `Lab - Payments Vulnerability Report`
** *Image type:* *Deployed images*
** *Namespace name:* `payments`
** *CVE severity:* *Critical*, *Important*
** *CVE status:* *Fixable*
** *Destinations* and *Schedule:* empty (`-`)
. Click *Save* (not *Create*). You land on the report configuration details page.

== Task 2.3: Generate and download on demand

. On the report details page, open *Actions*.
. Click *Generate download* (*Send report* stays disabled while *Delivery* has no destination).
+
image::s2/s2-02-report-actions.png[Actions menu showing Generate download for the saved report,width=720]
. Wait until the job completes under the *All report jobs* tab.
. Download the PDF and/or CSV when the run finishes.
. Open the file. The PDF lists CVEs grouped by image; the CSV contains columns such as CVE ID, severity, CVSS score, component, image name, and deployment. Both are scoped to the `payments` namespace.

== Task 2.4: Review the output

Use these questions to evaluate whether the report gives a tenant lead enough to prioritize fixes:

* How many fixable Important or Critical CVEs are in scope?
* Which image contributes the most critical fixable findings?
* Do any CVEs repeat across images (shared base layer)?

TIP: CSV output is useful for ticketing or SIEM import. Report configurations are also available through the RHACS API under `/v2/reports/configurations`.

== Key takeaways

* The create wizard is five steps: *Details* > *Resources* > *Filters* > *Delivery* > *Review*. Severity filters live on *Filters*, not on *Details*.
* *Custom scope* with a namespace chip is the current scoping model (collections are legacy).
* On-demand download (*Vulnerability Management* > *Reports* > open report > *Actions* > *Generate download*) works with an empty *Delivery* step. Scheduled email needs a notifier first.
* Scope by namespace so each tenant gets only their data. CSV output feeds ticketing and SIEM workflows.
