== Lab objectives

By completing this lab, participants will:

. Navigate the RHACS vulnerability dashboard and understand how Scanner V4 Indexer and Matcher produce scan results for base OS and language-level dependencies.
. Create automated vulnerability reports (PDF/CSV) scoped to a tenant namespace, using download or generate-now when SMTP is unavailable.
. Build Scoped Access Control (Permission Set, Access Scope, Role) and map a Keycloak OIDC group to that role for tenant isolation.
. Use the Risk view, policy scope exclusions, and CVE deferral to support SST and DSI prioritization workflows.
. Configure a generic webhook notifier (OpenITCockpit pattern) and inspect the ACS JSON payload.
. Integrate RHACS with Splunk via HEC for SIEM correlation of policy breach events.

== Day-2 workflow (discussion)

In production, these exercises chain together:

. A developer with a scoped role (Exercise 3) works only in their namespace.
. Risk and deferral (Exercise 4) decide what must be fixed now versus tracked with an exception.
. Webhook and Splunk (Exercises 5 and 6) carry the same policy event into monitoring and SIEM.
. Scheduled reports (Exercise 2) give tenant leads a recurring digest without manual CSV packs.
